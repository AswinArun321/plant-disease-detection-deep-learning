Image Preprocessing

Uploaded leaf images are sanitized, verified, resized to 224x224 RGB, and normalized to match the deep model input domain.

Deep Model Inference

Academic & Technical Foundation

Transfer Learning & Explainable AI (XAI)

Bridging the gap between black-box deep neural networks and actionable agricultural trust

🔬

Transfer Learning Architectures

Pretrained ImageNet backbones (MobileNetV2, ResNet50, EfficientNetB0) were empirically evaluated. The top layers are fine-tuned on PlantVillage to recognize subtle fungal spore patterns and bacterial leaf water-soaking.

🌟

Explainable AI (Grad-CAM)

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping computes the gradient of the predicted class score with respect to convolutional feature maps, visually revealing which leaf regions drove the model's prediction.

🛡️

Cautious AI Decision Support

AI should assist, not mislead. If prediction confidence drops below 60%, the system flags uncertainty and recommends on-field agricultural extension verification.
